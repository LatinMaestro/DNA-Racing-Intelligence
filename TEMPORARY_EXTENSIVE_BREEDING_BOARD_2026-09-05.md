## Evidence policy

This board follows the permanent performance-first breeding rule. A Core without credible breeder evidence is not an actionable breeding target merely because its element, F-number, family or local Trial percentile looks attractive. An unproven parent should first demonstrate population-elite exact-distance Bike speed. Local Trainer/Discovery percentiles are screening evidence only. Proven/repeat breeder evidence may justify a different route, but formal elite-breeder status still requires chronological offspring benchmarking.

Distance bands: Sprint 900–1400m; Middle 1500–1900m; Long 2000–2300m. Exact-distance alignment is preferred.
