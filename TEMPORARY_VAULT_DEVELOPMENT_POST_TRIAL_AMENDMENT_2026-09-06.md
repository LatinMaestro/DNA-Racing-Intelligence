## Durable lesson

The eight-child cohort demonstrates why the product must never label an offspring's distance from pedigree, stars or results alone. It must compare:

- intended distance before racing;
- actual exact-distance median/ceiling/consistency;
- global-relative evidence;
- sample depth;
- stars/field-relative evidence;
- adjacent-distance performance;
- and whether the child moved toward or away from the family's expected profile.

The first Trial evidence did **not** validate 1000 as the answer. Yellow-star support was encouraging, but central 1000 speed was not. This is exactly the kind of Core the system should **redirect rather than reject**.

## 4.2 Mario Kart × Starline -> Frost Rocket

This is currently the most interesting Mario offspring from a pure early Sprint-speed perspective.

Future test priority: obtain at least n≈5 at 1000, then build toward n≈10 if the signal survives. Test 1200 and 1400 afterward to determine whether she inherited Starline's short-distance breadth or Mario's 1400 emphasis.

# 5. Breeding doctrine updated by the completed cohort

## 5.1 A mating is not successful merely because the projection fills a structural gap

The child must become fast enough to occupy that role. Examples:

- Frozen Blade gives Earth F16 structurally, but the gap is not truly closed until Sprint performance validates.
- Frost Rocket gives Earth F15, but the useful outcome is the speed, not the F-number itself.
- Peak Crown gives Fire F12 and early strong 2200 performance; that combination is more valuable than a hypothetical Fire F18 that is merely average.

## 5.2 Exact-repeat success is probabilistic, not deterministic

BLNT × She Will Reign had prior elite-family evidence, but Iron Dynamo still needs to prove his own distance. Pair-repeat evidence should raise the probability distribution, not guarantee the child grade.

## 5.3 Use high-capacity breeders to learn; preserve scarce breeders for high-confidence decisions

The owner should be able to see:

- lifetime splices remaining;
- current-cycle splices remaining;
- quality and number of prior offspring;
- exact-pair repeat history;
- and the decision value of spending the next splice.

A 3-lifetime-splice Core and an effectively-unlimited Genesis sire must not be treated as equal resources.

## 5.4 Runner proof threshold for unproven breeders

If a parent lacks credible breeder evidence, require genuinely elite exact-distance racing evidence before promoting the pair. The working discovery doctrine has used approximately:

- at least n≈5 as the minimum useful signal;
- stronger confidence at n≈10 and n≈20;
- population-relative speed rather than vault-only rank;
- and consistency/ceiling/field evidence as supporting dimensions.

Do not call an unproven parent elite because it ranked highly in a small Trainer event cohort.

## 5.5 Repeated family analysis must use all children

Do not highlight only famous successful children. Pair/parent models need:

- all known offspring;
- offspring with too little evidence marked as unresolved rather than removed;
- median child quality;
- elite-tail rate;
- weak-child rate;
- distance inheritance distribution;
- variance;
- co-parent controls where possible;
- chronology/no-leakage controls when measuring parent lift.

# 7. Product requirements created by this amendment

The future website must support a full **breed -> hypothesis -> offspring -> discovery -> family-update** loop.

For every new child it should preserve:

1. parent pair and pair evidence at decision time;
2. intended target distance/category before racing;
3. structural gap the mating intended to fill;
4. projected element/F/type and official pair validation/cost snapshot;
5. first-race test plan;
6. exact-distance telemetry as it accumulates;
7. stars/results separately from speed;
8. agreement/conflict between evidence families;
9. distance redirects and why they occurred;
10. whether the child became roster-quality;
11. whether it truly closed the intended vault gap;
12. how the outcome updates sire breeder evidence;
13. how it updates dam breeder evidence;
14. how it updates the exact-pair model;
15. whether a repeat, related cross or stop decision is now justified.

# 8. Current gap interpretation after these births

Do not mark a structural gap as permanently solved by a birth alone. Track two states:

- **structural candidate exists**;
- **performance-qualified role is actually filled**.

Examples:

- Earth F16–20: Frozen Blade creates the structural candidate; Sprint performance still unresolved.
- Earth F11–15: Frost Rocket / Cyber Overdrive create candidates; role quality must be confirmed at the right Sprint distances.
- Fire F11–15: Peak Crown F12 now has strong 2200 evidence and is therefore much closer to a genuinely filled performance gap.
- Fire F16–20: still a major strategic target unless another existing Core already fills it on performance after live revalidation.
- non-Metal 1000–1200 F11+: Frost Rocket is a possible solution if her Sprint signal survives additional testing.

This two-stage gap model is a core website requirement.
