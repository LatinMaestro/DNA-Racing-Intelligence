# Phase 6 Offspring Cost Basis

## Purpose

Prepare an optional, auditable assignment of actual pairing costs to the owned
offspring produced by a confirmed completed breeding event.

## Contract

- Accept only confirmed actual DNA, external-owner and BGC pairing costs.
- Require confirmed offspring ownership and a completed breeding event.
- Keep every original asset separate and preserve exact decimal amounts.
- Link refunds to an included cost in the same asset and prevent over-refunds.
- Prevent one economic transaction from being assigned to more than one cost
  basis.
- Hold proposed, reversed, missing or duplicate evidence for review.
- Preserve transaction, event, offspring and refund provenance.

## Boundaries

Cost-basis assignment is optional and review-only. It does not infer a value for
the offspring, combine BGC with cash or crypto, calculate a realised gain, or
mutate the ledger automatically.
