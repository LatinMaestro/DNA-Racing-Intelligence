# Phase 9 Hosted Explainability Attestations

## Boundary

This evidence-only contract binds recommendation explainability and audit
traceability to one exact candidate head and reviewed synthetic fixtures. It
does not claim analytical success, connect providers, activate recommendations
or operate the game.

Required controls cover evidence, sample size, confidence, uncertainty,
freshness, coverage, rule/model provenance, feature contributions,
alternatives, tradeoffs, mode and distance context, experimental labelling,
chronological no-leakage cutoffs and explicit partial or unavailable states.

## Evidence rules

- Bind every result to one exact head and reviewed manifests.
- Expose evidence, limitations, provenance and chronological cutoffs.
- Keep confirmed rules distinct from inferred or experimental findings.
- Preserve mode, exact distance, sample and freshness context.
- Show alternatives and tradeoffs without hiding stronger evidence.
- Fail closed when evidence, cost basis, coverage or capability is incomplete.
- Keep all recommendations review-only and every game action unavailable.
- Use synthetic fixtures only and retain no private artifact.
- Record exact UTC bounds, complete assertions and redacted summary digests.
- Block stale heads, manifest drift, partial checks and unsafe evidence.

## Authority

The projection cannot validate private analytical performance, dispatch Actions,
merge, connect providers, expose routes, operate the game or mutate Production.
Formal exact-head and connected evidence remain required.
