# Phase 9 Hosted Game-Rule Attestations

## Boundary

This evidence-only contract binds every mandatory owner-confirmed product rule
to one exact candidate head, one reviewed rule manifest and one synthetic
fixture manifest. It does not change game rules, connect to DNA Racing or claim
live state.

Required controls cover metres, Gold gate eligibility, Open Race star timing,
the 50% gate cap, Maiden mode preservation, separate breeding upside and
vault-gap rankings, durable-ID lineage, historical BGC zero economics,
asset-separated accounting with transfer exclusion, Genesis burn exclusion,
configurable qualification and the ten-race Discovery minimum.

## Evidence rules

- Use only owner-confirmed rules and reviewed fixed command identities.
- Bind every result to one exact head and reviewed rule/fixture manifests.
- Verify positive, boundary and negative cases for every control.
- Never replace a confirmed rule with source inference or model output.
- Preserve rule provenance and mode/distance context.
- Never describe imported snapshots or recommendations as live state.
- Keep every recommendation review-only and every game action unavailable.
- Use synthetic fixtures only and retain no private artifact.
- Record exact UTC bounds, complete assertions and redacted summary digests.
- Block stale heads, manifest drift, partial checks and unsafe evidence.

## Authority

The projection cannot change a confirmed rule, dispatch Actions, merge, connect
providers, expose routes, operate the game or mutate Production. Formal
exact-head execution and review remain required after Actions capacity returns.
