## Existing method boundaries that must not be weakened

- **Core performance:** exact mode and distance, comparable format/gates where relevant, source seconds → milliseconds correctly, time/speed direction, median/trimmed mean/distribution, variance, sample size, freshness, and quality/coverage of benchmark population. Do not turn missing observations into poor performance.
- **Discovery:** V2 central pace, ceiling, repeatability, stars, opposition, format/gate transfer and sample-aware archetypes, plus targeted side-distance/two-band rule-out. Normal-Free target of 20 is a study objective, distinct from 10-race minimally analytical status. Strong stars in a deliberate 2–5 race elite benchmark screen have a **specific**, limited interpretation; outside that context mature exact-format time remains primary. Never automatically bench/promote on missing stars.
- **Qualification:** hard-filter eligibility first; compute the *configured* objective, not a generic wins score. Fastest-time uses best-of-N/lower-tail potential; median and mean use the actual configured aggregation; points/wins/top-X/best-finish/custom require confirmed scoring and appropriate simulations or exact rule calculation. Separate likely tournament-strength opponent populations by mode, distance, format, gate, eligibility and leaderboard group; retain owner element-specific splits.
- **Pro League:** Bike-only, own published maps and line rules. Do not leak Car/Horse historical results into roster selection or blindly share normal-tournament economics and weights.
- **Maiden:** preserve one-use ME opportunity and compare credible value across modes; do not conflate provisional selection with game entry.
- **Open Race:** eligibility and pre-entry ranking use only pre-entry information; after field lock, later stars cannot rewrite the frozen recommendation.
- **Breeding:** do not assert that the hidden inheritance formula is known. Separate racer ability from offspring lift relative to the pre-creation mating baseline; require sufficiently sampled, chronologically valid offspring evidence, confidence and partner diversity.
- **Lifecycle:** no automatic sell/burn/race action. Never use unverified burn credit, missing cost basis, incomplete stars or unsupported profit inference to justify an irreversible decision.
- **Economics:** preserve original asset/currency and exact decimals, distinguish BGC, distinguish transactions from operational profit, treat missing acquisition cost and incomplete payouts as unknown, and do not silently translate price or listing into realised proceeds.
- **Across every module:** historical snapshots are not guaranteed live; show data-current-through, last-sync, coverage, eligible sample count, confidence, missing/partial evidence, calibration status, provenance and advisory limitations.

## Mandatory feature-delivery process

Before any analytical work in app/, components/, domain/, lib/, API routes or persistence:

1. **Audit** this matrix, AGENTS.md, relevant owner Issue #120 amendments, applicable docs and the existing domain/lib implementation, UI/service wiring, migrations/read models and all related tests. Inspect current-main source rather than historical branch snippets.
2. **Trace each input and mathematical decision** from accepted source → normalized fact → domain method → persistence/read model → service → owner-facing UI. Create a compact per-feature traceability checklist stating each existing rule, where implemented, test evidence, version, and any gap.
3. **Reuse or extend** the established domain function. Do not fork formulas, add weights, sample gates, percentile cutoffs, confidence thresholds or economic assumptions simply to complete UI.
4. **When authority is missing or contradictory**, record the exact question and sources; keep uncertain inputs or results review-only/unavailable until confirmed. Only introduce a novel model with an explicit documented gap, sound evidence, and owner approval for material strategy changes; version the method and write focused comparisons against accepted baseline behaviour.
5. **Test** multiple modes, exact-distance isolation, field/element/format partitions, stale/partial/unknown outcomes, sample-size boundaries, direction/units, ties, fees/assets, point-in-time leakage and historical benchmarks as relevant. Do not change a previously approved decision outcome without explicit regression evidence and rationale.
6. **Do not claim fully commissioned** merely because a formula has a unit test or synthetic UI. Confirm linked private data and lifecycle acceptance when provider quota allows; otherwise label connected verification pending.

No game-state action, paid provider operation or live Neon transfer is authorised by this methodology index.
