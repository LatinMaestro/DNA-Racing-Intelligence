# Phase 7 Core-Sale Evidence

## Purpose

Translate a confirmed completed core sale into exact review postings without
turning strategic lifecycle advice into execution evidence.

## Contract

- Require confirmed sale evidence and confirmed active ownership at the event.
- Preserve sale proceeds and selling fees in their original asset.
- Calculate realised result only when acquisition cost and every fee use the
  same asset as the proceeds.
- Keep proceeds visible but mark realised result unavailable when cost basis is
  missing.
- Refuse to combine unlike assets or infer a market value.

## Boundaries

The contract does not list, transfer or sell a core. It does not mutate current
ownership, post a hosted ledger entry or treat a prior `sell` recommendation as
proof that a sale occurred. Every output remains a review proposal.
