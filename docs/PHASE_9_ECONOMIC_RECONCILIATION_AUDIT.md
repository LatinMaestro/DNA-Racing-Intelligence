# Phase 9 Economic Reconciliation Audit

This contract recalculates operating income, expense and net with exact decimal
arithmetic and compares the results with supplied asset-separated report totals.

It blocks inclusion of deposits, withdrawals, transfers, opening balances and
reconciliation movements in operating P/L. BGC remains separate from cash and
crypto, and unlike assets cannot be silently combined. Duplicate links,
unlinked manual tournament payouts, unresolved records, conversion coverage and
core-sale cost basis remain explicit audit issues.

The audit cannot mutate the ledger, establish dependable totals from synthetic
evidence or accept Gate C. A claimed complete report is rejected whenever
material coverage or reconciliation evidence remains unresolved.
