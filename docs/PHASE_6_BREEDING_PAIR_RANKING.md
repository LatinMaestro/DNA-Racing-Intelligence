# Phase 6 Breeding Pair Ranking

## Purpose

Present breeding opportunities on the three approved dimensions without allowing
vault saturation to hide a rare high-upside pairing.

## Contract

- Publish separate elite-upside, vault-gap and balanced rankings.
- Rank elite upside first by exceptional-outcome probability and never by vault
  fit.
- Rank vault-gap improvement independently from elite upside.
- Calculate the balanced view from explicit integer basis-point weights.
- Preserve exact mode, distance, parent identity, confidence and evidence
  cutoffs.
- Hold stale, unavailable, rule-unresolved, uncalibrated or unsupported
  star-enhanced evidence.
- Reject duplicate parent pairs regardless of parent order.

## Boundaries

The rankings consume already-audited evidence and do not discover rules,
calibrate a model or mutate breeding state. They remain experimental and cannot
authorise a pairing or breeding transaction before Gate E.
