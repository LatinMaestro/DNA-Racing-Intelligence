# Phase 6 Breeding Economic Evidence

## Purpose

Translate confirmed completed breeding evidence into auditable, review-only
economic postings while preventing arena listings from becoming false income.

## Contract

- Accept completed evidence only from an authoritative transaction export or a
  reasoned manual confirmation.
- Keep DNA fees, external-owner fees, BGC fees, earned fees and refunds
  explicitly categorised.
- Preserve exact decimal amounts and report every asset separately.
- Require BGC to remain a separate game-credit asset.
- Hold failed, pending or unknown breeding activity without postings.
- Restrict refunded records to confirmed credit refunds.
- Preserve parent, offspring, transaction and external-reference provenance.

## Boundaries

An arena listing is availability and nominated-fee evidence only. It cannot
create income, prove a completed splice or mutate the ledger. The contract
produces review proposals and cannot initiate a wallet, game or ledger action.
