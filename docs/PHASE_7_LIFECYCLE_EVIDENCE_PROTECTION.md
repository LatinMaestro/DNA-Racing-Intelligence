# Phase 7 Lifecycle Evidence Protection

## Purpose

Protect unresolved or strategically valuable cores before any race, sell or burn
advice is composed.

## Contract

- Keep inactive cores as historical evidence only.
- Permanently prohibit burning Genesis cores.
- Protect eligible, unknown or invalid Maiden evidence.
- Protect promising or unresolved Discovery evidence.
- Protect supported or unresolved racing, breeding and lineage value.
- Hold stale and incomplete evidence for review.
- Distinguish non-star negative evidence from no-star and Gold-ineligible
  absence.
- Never allow no-star or Gold-ineligible evidence alone to support disposal.

## Boundaries

The contract does not choose a final lifecycle action, mutate ownership, sell or
burn a core, value a burn credit or post a ledger entry. A structurally
reviewable spliced core remains review-only pending composed evidence and the
applicable analytical gate.
