# Phase 9 Star-Algorithm Era Detection

This contract compares adjacent, non-overlapping periods for the same mode and
exact distance. It uses explicit assignment and outcome counts, exact
basis-point rates, minimum denominators and material-shift thresholds.

It keeps assignment-frequency shifts separate from Gold top-three and Blue win
conversion shifts. Partial periods and inadequate denominators remain
insufficient evidence.

A material shift creates a review candidate only. The contract cannot establish
that the hidden algorithm changed, identify a cause, segment authoritative eras
automatically or accept Gate C. Synthetic fixtures validate arithmetic and
fail-closed behaviour only.
