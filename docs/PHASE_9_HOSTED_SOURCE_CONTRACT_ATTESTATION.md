# Phase 9 Hosted Source-Contract Attestations

## Boundary

This evidence-only contract binds all nine authoritative inputs and their update
semantics to one exact candidate head, reviewed source contracts, a reviewed
aggregate profile and synthetic fixtures. It does not import private files,
activate a source version or claim connected persistence.

Required controls cover cross-mode Core Details, confirmed-owned Current Vault
and ME states, replacement Current Arena snapshots, six ordered Race Merge
additions, durable-ID resolution, periodic update semantics, privacy-safe
aggregate coverage, historical BGC zero economics with retained provenance and
the approved private raw-data boundary.

## Evidence rules

- Bind every result to one exact head and reviewed manifests.
- Verify all nine source roles and grouped sequential Race Merge ordering.
- Treat Core Details as cross-mode and relationships as durable-ID facts only.
- Treat supplied Vault rows as owned and preserve ME true/false separately.
- Treat Vault and Arena as replacement historical snapshots.
- Preserve source values and every analytically relevant field privately.
- Keep Git, routine logs and public surfaces redacted.
- Preserve historical BGC race performance while materialising zero economics.
- Use synthetic fixtures only and retain no private artifact.
- Block stale heads, manifest drift, partial checks and unsafe evidence.

## Authority

The projection cannot upload or activate data, mutate providers, dispatch
Actions, merge, expose routes or mutate Production. Connected import,
persistence and private real-file evidence remain separately required.
