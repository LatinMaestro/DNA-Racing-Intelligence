# Phase 9 hosted analytical validation attestations

Status: private-owner evidence contract; no validation execution claimed.

## Purpose

Bind privacy-safe chronological validation evidence to one exact repository head
and source manifest across:

- performance distributions;
- lineage lift;
- breeding validation;
- Gold/Blue era detection;
- tournament and Maiden calibration;
- recommendation calibration;
- capacity projection; and
- economic reconciliation.

Every analysis uses a reviewed command identifier, historical cutoff, exact UTC
execution bounds, aggregate-summary digest and documented coverage. Execution
must occur in the authenticated owner workspace. Retained evidence is aggregate
only, remains experimentally labelled and contains no private source rows.

Any future leakage, stale head, source drift, failed command, incomplete
coverage or row-level retained evidence blocks validation. Historical Race Merge
BGC economics and internal transfers must be excluded from economic
reconciliation.

Only two unavailable/limited evidence codes are accepted: missing breeding
timestamps for breeding validation and missing point-in-time ME history for
Maiden calibration. They remain explicit limitations rather than inferred
facts.

The projection cannot dispatch Actions, merge, publish private artifacts or
mutate Production.
