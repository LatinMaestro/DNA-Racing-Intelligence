# Phase 9 Pro League breeding objectives

Verified high- and medium-priority Pro League coverage gaps may now be translated
into bounded breeding research objectives. The objective layer consumes the
active exact-format roster generation and existing held-Gate-E breeding
rankings; it does not create a new breeding model.

- Only current or ageing Bike evidence at the exact gap distance is eligible.
- Race-type-specific pair performance remains unavailable and is disclosed on
  every candidate. Car, Horse, adjacent-distance and stale evidence cannot fill
  a Pro League objective.
- Vault-gap and elite-upside research views remain visible together so Vault fit
  cannot suppress an exceptional hypothesis.
- Existing family, sex, cycle, splice-capacity, availability, chronology and
  distribution gates remain upstream prerequisites.
- Current official `pair_validate` and `pair_info` evidence is still required at
  owner decision time. Arena state must also be revalidated when an external
  parent is involved.
- Gate E remains held. Candidates are decision-support research, not pairing
  recommendations or predictions of deterministic offspring quality.

The objective queue cannot call the DNA API, validate a pair automatically,
connect a wallet, execute a splice, create an economic record or mutate the Pro
League roster.
