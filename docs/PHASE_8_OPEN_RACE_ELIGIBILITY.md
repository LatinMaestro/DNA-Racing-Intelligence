# Phase 8 Open Race Eligibility

## Purpose

Filter the current owned Vault against confirmed manually entered Open Race
restrictions before any performance ranking occurs.

## Contract

- Require confirmed active ownership, availability and complete core attributes.
- Apply only confirmed class, element, F-number and Maiden restrictions.
- Keep uncertain rules, stale Vault evidence and unresolved identity or
  attributes review-required without applying uncertain restrictions as
  definitive exclusions.
- Treat confirmed non-ownership, manual unavailability and rule mismatch as
  ineligible.
- Preserve eligible, ineligible and review-required outcomes separately.

## Boundaries

The contract does not rank cores, use current-race stars, alter ownership or ME,
reserve a gate, enter a race or infer an unconfirmed restriction.
