# DNA Pro Esports League Preparation

## Status and authority

This document records the owner-supplied DNA Community Update received on 20 August 2026. It is the complete published information currently available to the owner about the new DNA Pro Esports League.

Treat the published facts below as provisional official announcement evidence. Do not invent missing rules. If later DNA announcements conflict with this document, update the ruleset version, preserve the earlier version for provenance, and re-evaluate any roster or breeding recommendation that depends on the changed rule.

The owner intends to participate and wants the private website to prepare for a Pro-level roster from breeding, Discovery and team-core selection perspectives.

## Owner strategy

- Do **not** recommend or plan participation in the special Genesis mint.
- The owner considers the existing Genesis/core inventory sufficient.
- Build the Esports roster from the current owner-maintained Vault plus breeding.
- Prefer breeding as the mechanism for creating additional roster depth, especially where non-Genesis, element or F15+ coverage is weak.
- Do not add wallet, minting, marketplace or automated game actions to the website.

This owner strategy overrides the announcement's general suggestion to prepare mint funds.

## Published Esports facts

DNA's announcement states:

- Esports is a separate mode with its own UI and systems.
- Cores are the connection between DNA Racing and Esports and may be used in both.
- Other systems are described as separate.
- The current Esports focus is Bike.
- There will be 12 Pro teams.
- Pro players are paid to compete and do not pay entry or badge fees.
- Players may secure their own sponsors.
- Teams may have custom home and away skins.
- An open-entry lower league is planned without a stated team-count limit.
- Seasonal promotion and relegation move the top three lower-league teams into Pro and the bottom three Pro teams down.
- Pro matches are planned twice weekly, Tuesday and Friday nights at 9:00 PM ET.
- Each match is best-of-three maps.
- A map is first to 16, win by two, with up to 42 races.
- Live betting markets are planned for races. Betting functionality is outside this website's preparation scope.
- Internal testing is already underway.
- The current public-access estimate is 1 September 2026, but DNA explicitly says it is not rushing launch.

## Published roster requirements

The announcement says a competitive Esports roster needs:

- 25 cores total;
- minimum 5 Metal;
- minimum 5 Fire;
- minimum 5 Earth;
- minimum 5 Water;
- maximum 2 "gens" per element;
- minimum 8 females; and
- at least 5 F15+ cores.

### Working interpretation of "gens"

The website currently interprets "maximum 2 gens per element" as a maximum of two **Genesis** cores per element because the same announcement discusses Genesis as the Esports base pieces and the repository's core-class vocabulary uses `Genesis`.

Keep this labelled as a working interpretation until DNA formally expands or confirms the shorthand. Do not silently convert it into a permanent confirmed rule.

## Analytical boundary: DNA Racing is not Esports evidence

The announcement explicitly says the Esports UI and systems are separate. Therefore:

- existing DNA Racing Bike times, exact-distance profiles, Gold/Blue signals and lineage evidence may be used as **prior evidence for triage and testing only**;
- they must not be presented as measured Esports ability;
- the website must not assume DNA Racing exact-distance strengths transfer to Esports maps;
- the website must not assume the DNA Racing Gold/Blue algorithm exists in Esports;
- a final Pro roster must not be ranked as authoritative from DNA Racing data alone; and
- when real Esports results or a supported export/API become available, store and analyse them as a separate evidence family unless DNA later confirms shared mechanics.

Any future model that uses DNA Racing evidence to predict Esports outcomes requires chronological validation against actual Esports results before the relationship is represented as dependable.

## Roster preparation model

The Esports Prep workspace should separate four questions.

### 1. Structural readiness

Use current owner-maintained My Vault state plus Core Details to show:

- total owned cores;
- count by element;
- Genesis and non-Genesis count by element;
- female count;
- F15+ count; and
- how many cores remain selectable if the working two-Genesis-per-element cap is applied.

Pool-level structural checks are not the same as validating an exact 25-core selected roster. When manual roster selection is added, validate the selected roster itself against all published constraints.

### 2. Breeding preparation

Breeding remains probabilistic for racing quality, but confirmed structural inheritance can support roster planning:

- offspring F-number is the sum of parent F-numbers, so a planned F15+ offspring requires parent F-numbers summing to at least 15;
- offspring takes the lower-ranked parent element, so use the confirmed element inheritance rule to target an element;
- bred cores are non-Genesis and therefore expand roster flexibility under a Genesis cap;
- no confirmed rule lets the website deterministically target offspring sex, so female roster gaps must be managed through offspring outcomes and retention rather than a claimed female-producing pairing; and
- parent racing quality should continue to use the existing elite-upside, Vault-gap and balanced breeding methodology, with Bike evidence receiving strategic attention for Esports preparation but not being mislabelled as Esports performance.

Deterministic element guidance from the existing breeding rules:

- Metal target: Metal × Metal.
- Fire target: Fire × Fire or Metal × Fire.
- Earth target: at least one Earth parent and no Water parent.
- Water target: any otherwise eligible pairing involving a Water parent.

### 3. Discovery preparation

Discovery keeps its existing owner-Vault meaning. For Esports preparation:

- give strategic priority to **Bike** hypotheses for cores that could fill scarce roster roles;
- prioritise under-tested females, F15+ cores and non-Genesis depth where those groups are needed for roster construction;
- prioritise element groups with fewer than five credible Bike-prior candidates;
- continue using direct exact-distance evidence first, then lineage and population support;
- keep the 10-race exact-distance boundary as the minimum for a minimally analytical DNA Racing sample;
- do not race randomly merely to create Esports activity; and
- do not assume any DNA Racing distance is an Esports map requirement until DNA publishes map mechanics or Esports evidence demonstrates the relationship.

### 4. Team-core selection

Before Esports results exist, the website may rank **evidence readiness** but not definitive Esports ability.

The preparation view should group candidate cores by element and show:

- structural traits relevant to the roster rules;
- DNA Racing Bike race count and exact-distance sample depth;
- whether at least one Bike exact distance has a minimally analytical sample;
- freshness of the prior evidence; and
- reasons a core is a high-priority Discovery candidate.

A later exact roster-selection workflow should allow the owner to choose 25 cores and run deterministic compliance validation. Performance ranking should become Esports-specific only when supported by actual Esports evidence.
